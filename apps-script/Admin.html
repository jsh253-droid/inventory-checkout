Offices

Each office is a tab on the check-out site. It reads items from the sheet tab you pick, starting at "first row" (2 = just below the header). The tab's Total Out formula tells the site which Form Responses columns belong to it. A "link only" office has no tab button; open it by adding ?office= and its name to the site link, e.g. ?office=Warehouse.
